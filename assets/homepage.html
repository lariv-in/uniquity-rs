Advancing India's Autonomous Future.

Uniquity Ventures supplies industrial machinery across India. Backed by a diverse, multi-category fleet, we support operations across infrastructure, manufacturing, commercial facilities, and construction.

Our Mission

Put industrial machinery within reach of every construction site and factory in India.

Builders and manufacturers should not have to buy a fleet, staff a maintenance crew, or wait on equipment before work can start. Uniquity Ventures lends industrial machinery across the country and stays responsible for the machine for as long as the client needs it.

On our end we transport it, install it, maintain it, and dismantle it. On the commercial side we match the hire to the job: regular rent, EMI that ends in ownership, a turn-key setup from requirement to upkeep, or one-time contract job work on our premises or at a select client's site.

Access without ownership

The right machine should be available without a large capital outlay. Clients pay for use, not for a fleet they have to fund, store, and eventually dispose of.

Uptime we stand behind

A hired machine that sits idle is a delayed project. We deploy and support equipment so crews can keep working, with a 90% machinery uptime guarantee.

The operational burden stays with us

Sourcing, transport, installation, maintenance, and dismantling are our work. Clients specify what they need to get done; we put a running machine in front of them.

Construction Sites

Active sites need machines that can move with the job: facade work, cladding, high-access operations, and general site work. We deliver, install, and keep the equipment running so crews are not waiting on a machine, and we stay responsible for upkeep for as long as the hire lasts.

Buildings

Standing buildings need access equipment for retrofit, facade maintenance, interior fit-out, and work at height without shutting the property down. We place the right machine on site, install it, and take it away when the work is finished, so building owners do not have to fund or store a fleet.

Factories

We lend to factories when the shop floor needs extra capacity or a specialized machine they do not want to purchase outright. We deliver, install, and support the machine so production can continue without a large capital outlay or a new maintenance crew.

Manufacturing Plants

Larger plants need equipment for line expansion, plant maintenance, and jobs that sit outside the installed base. We match the machine to the process, put it in place, and keep it running so the plant does not stop work to buy, commission, or staff another asset.

We lend machinery using

Choose the commercial model that fits the job: regular rental, a path to ownership, a fully managed setup, or one-time use of a machine.

Traditional Lending

The client pays rent on a regular schedule for as long as they need the machine. On our end we transport it to site, install it, maintain it through the hire, and dismantle it when the work is finished. Ownership stays with us; the client's obligation is the rent.

EMI Based

The client pays rent for a specified term. When that term ends, ownership of the machine transfers to the client. This is for operations that want to spread cost over time and eventually own the asset, without a large purchase up front.

Turn Key Solutions

The client specifies what they need to achieve. We recommend the right machine, procure it, install it, and maintain it. They do not have to source equipment, manage vendors, or run a maintenance programme—we remove that work from their plate.

Contract Job Work

For a one-time job, the client operates the required machine on our premises, or at a select client's premises we make available. There is no long-term hire and no machine sitting idle afterward: they use it for the job, then they are done.
